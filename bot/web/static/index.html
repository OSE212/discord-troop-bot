Troop Command — Setup Guide & Commands

Everything you need to set up the bot for your alliance, manage permissions, and run battle formations.

Invite Bot to Discord
STEP 1

1. Invite & Grant Roles

Click the Invite Bot button above to add Troop Command to your Discord server. Then create or assign two roles to your leadership:

  • Troop Admin: Grants full management (edit players, import CSVs, change rules). Anyone with Discord Administrator or Manage Server automatically qualifies.
  • Troop Calculator: Allows alliance leaders (R4/R5) to trigger /calculate formations.
STEP 2

2. Player Registration

Members register their troop and hero progression in Discord:

/register

Opens an interactive modal to enter In-Game Name, March Limit, FC levels (Infantry, Lancers, Marksman), and Helios counts.

/update

Used whenever an account upgrades (e.g. unlocking new FC tier, obtaining more Helios, or leveling heroes).

STEP 3

3. Optimize Battle Formations

When planning a Rally or Garrison:

/calculate

Prompts for Attack/Defence, Rally/Garrison, troop ratios, and total capacity. Calculates the optimal troop composition and identifies the Top 4 Joiners to maximize active skills.

Discord Slash Commands Reference

Command Permissions Description
/register All Members Guided 2-step modal to register your In-Game Name, March Limit, and Troop stats.
/update All Members Update your existing registered stats, FC levels, Helios counts, or hero progression.
/profile All Members Display your personal registered troop card and hero levels.
/calculate Troop Calculator Calculate an optimized Rally or Garrison formation with troop allocation breakdown and Top 4 Joiners.
/rules Troop Admin View current optimizer weights (Helios multiplier, FC level priority order).
